01 / PROFILE

Across disciplines.
Closer to practice.

My career spans Tokyo Women’s Medical University, Harvard Medical School/Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston, and Hitachi, Ltd., leading to my current role at Institute of Science Tokyo. I bring clinical engineering research and industry experience together to advance medical technologies through collaboration, spanning surgical assistance, nursing, hospital operations and patient-centered solutions.

At Hitachi, I contributed to the commercial launch of two medical products and gained experience in new business development, strategy and project management. This informs an approach that considers both technical feasibility and sustainable business, from R&D to real-world use.

02 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Engineering better care.

Starting with questions from clinical practice. Developing technologies for real-world use.

My research spans value for patients, the working lives of healthcare professionals, and improvements in hospital management and operations—considering both the people who receive and deliver care and the systems that support them.

01 / Medical Robotics

People and robots, working together

Investigating scrub nurse robots and surgical assistance that fits the movements and workflows of healthcare professionals.

02 / Connected Healthcare

Connecting devices and information

Investigating how connected medical devices and data can support clinical decisions and collaboration across medical and nursing teams.

03 / Surgical Data Science

Turning experience into insight

Exploring operating room data cruising, surgical workflow analysis and performance evaluation to make clinical experience visible.

04 / Nursing Innovation

Supporting nursing practice

Studying nursing workflows and the roles of people and robots to develop supportive technologies and environments for nursing professionals.

05 / Hospital Management

Quality of care and hospital operations

Using data on workflows and resource use to explore improvements in quality of care, hospital management and operations.

06 / Patient-Centered Solutions

Starting with the patient’s perspective

Investigating devices and services informed by patients’ experiences and everyday challenges, with the person receiving care at the center.

Clinical needs→Engineering & data→Translation

How we can work together

Exploring medical applications for your technology?

We can examine the fit between technical capabilities and clinical needs, frame joint research questions and identify issues to consider on the path to commercialization.

Turning challenges and questions from clinical practice into improvements

Everyday medical, nursing and hospital work can raise questions: “This is difficult” or “We have always done it this way, but could it be better?” We can start by understanding the problem and explore how technology or changes to the way work is organized might help.

Have a research idea you would like to explore together?

We can bring together research ideas and observations from practice to frame questions and approaches. Conversations about joint research are welcome before a detailed plan has taken shape.

Shaping a new healthcare business strategy or R&D direction

We can examine clinical challenges, your technology and strengths, and value for patients, healthcare professionals and institutions to explore new business strategy and research priorities with a path toward real-world use.

Looking for a speaker on healthcare innovation?

Talks and seminars can explore medical device innovation, medical robotics and development driven by clinical needs, with topics shaped around your audience and purpose.
