01 / PROFILE

Across disciplines.
Closer to practice.

My career spans Tokyo Women’s Medical University, Harvard Medical School/Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston, and Hitachi, Ltd., leading to my current role at Institute of Science Tokyo. I bring clinical engineering research and industry experience together to advance medical technologies through collaboration, spanning surgical assistance, nursing, hospital operations and patient-centered solutions.

At Hitachi, I contributed to the commercial launch of two medical products and gained experience in new business development, strategy and project management. This informs an approach that considers both technical feasibility and sustainable business, from R&D to real-world use.

Bringing clinical and business perspectives to industry R&D: exploring medical applications of technology, shaping development priorities and considering pathways toward commercialization.

01 / Medical Robotics

People and robots, working together

02 / Connected Healthcare

Connecting devices and information

Investigating how connected medical devices and data can support clinical decisions and collaboration across medical and nursing teams.

03 / Surgical Data Science

Turning experience into insight

Exploring operating room data cruising, surgical workflow analysis and performance evaluation to make clinical experience visible.

Supporting nursing practice

05 / Hospital Management

Quality of care and hospital operations

Using data on workflows and resource use to explore improvements in quality of care, hospital management and operations.
